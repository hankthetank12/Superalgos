- **Consensus (§8 ladder, resolved once at scoping):** (1) if the workbook has a VAActuals/consensus tab → **red links** everywhere consensus appears — the only source that supports build-time tie verification (VA whole-percent ratios `/100`); (2) else a **red hardcoded snapshot** from what the user pastes, values + as-of date stated in the reply; (3) **Bloomberg BDP/BDH written only as dormant refresh-on-open formulas on the 1 Pager / NTM PE** — this environment is Bloomberg-dead, so they are never the Model's spine and never claimed verified.

- **EPS basis rule (§11):** BX consensus is on **Distributable EPS**; the EPS strip and all seeding run on DE/share, matched to the consensus basis before any GAAP bridge.

## 5. Engines to build for BX specifically (alt-manager-engine layers, per segment)
Tier-1 sections mirror management's cut and order from the 2Q26 release — expected: **Real Estate, Private Equity, Credit & Insurance, Multi-Asset Investing** (confirm against the release; take exactly what BX prints).
- **Layer 1a — flagship drawdown fund blocks** (~40–50 rows each, tier-3 banners): every active + in-market flagship from the release's fund table — expected BREP X / BREP Europe VII, BCP IX, Strategic Partners IX, BXG II, energy-transition vintages (BETP/BGREEN), Tactical Opportunities, BXLS V — **plus next vintages management has guided to**. Each block: investment-period vs step-down fee-rate inputs; commitments walk (new closes seeded to fundraising targets); deployment walk with dry powder; realizations walk; NAV walk with MOIC; **binary blue step-down trigger** switching the fee basis (commitments → invested) and rate; fee holidays modeled as delayed activation per guided dates; fund mgmt fees = FPAUM × rate / 4.
- **Layer 1b — perpetual/semi-liquid vehicles** (BX's defining fee base, ~$450bn+): one row per vehicle — BREIT, BPP, BIP/infrastructure, BCRED, BXSL, BXPE, BEPIF, insurance SMAs (Corebridge, F&G, Everlake, Resolution Life) — EOP AUM grown by blue %/NNA inputs, per-vehicle fee-rate rows, SUMPRODUCT blended rate.
- **Layer 2 — segment FEAUM walks** tying BX's reported bridges: BOP + inflows − outflows − realizations ± market ± step-downs = EOP; flagship/perpetual flows green-linked from Layer 1 with a red non-flagship residual; average FEAUM (BX reports the fee-basis average — blue in actuals, AVERAGE in estimates with the diff noted); `Memo: Total AUM` and **Perpetual Capital AUM** ties; violet guidance rows attach here (fundraising targets, activation timing).
- **Layer 3 — segment PnL:** blended fee-rate stack; **base management fees** with the base-fee-rate check row (fees ×4 / avg FPAUM vs the stack); transaction & advisory fees; fee offsets; **fee-related performance revenues** on a crystallization schedule (BREIT/BCRED annual 4Q; institutional infrastructure ~3-yr cycle — schedule memo rows); fee-related comp ratio and opex %Y/Y inputs → **FRE + FRE margin** bands.
- **Layer 4 — net realizations:** realized performance revenues tied to the funds' realization walks; realized perf comp (% input); realized principal investment income → Total net realizations, with the crystallization `Memo:` calendar.
- **Layer 5 — group rollup + unit structure:** Σ segment DE → interest & other → taxes (blue ETR) → **DE**; **unit waterfall** (BX keeps Blackstone Holdings partnership units): `% to Common = common shares / total units`, partnership units as the red residual → **DE/share**; dividend = policy % of DE (~85%, blue input) with payout-ratio row; buyback share roll; **Capital & Returns module (§19)** with FRE/DE per-share rows replacing EPS.
- **Company-only KPI drivers mined at Step 0** (disclosed by BX, absent from consensus — flagged as such in the reply): **net accrued performance revenues** ($/share store-of-value memo), per-fund **dry powder / % called / MOIC**, **fundraising-toward-target** trackers, **BREIT/BCRED monthly returns & NAV** via a G6 staging tab + G7 Seasonals/Vs-Normal as the intra-quarter nowcast lens, % of perf-eligible AUM above hurdle memo.
- **Cross-cutting model-engine modules:** Summary IS of dark-green links; monthly SPX block feeding market-appreciation inputs (§12/§14 discipline — AUM never forecast on a bare %Y/Y); Incremental Margins row; check rows at every seam (Σ segment FEAUM = total; Σ segment DE = consolidated; every walk BOP = prior EOP); one fixed SUMIFS range for all annual columns.
