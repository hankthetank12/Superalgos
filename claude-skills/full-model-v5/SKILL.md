## When to use
Full quarterly earnings model for a public company — a single "Model" sheet with quarterly history + forecasts, monthly detail, driver engines below the P&L, Street formatting — optionally followed IN THE SAME BUILD by its Mini Model, Revisions, Drivers, and Qtr tabs. Trigger phrases: "build the full model", "quarterly model", "earnings model", "model this like IBKR", "model this like HOOD/BULL", "retail broker model". For annual-only mini-models on their own use the `va-mini-model` / `mini-model-*` skills. When someone wants ONLY a Mini Model, Revisions, Drivers, or Qtr tab added to a model that already exists, the standalone `mini-model-v7-8-26` / `revisions-tab` / `drivers-kpi-vs-consensus-v2` / `qtr-model-vs-street-v2` skills are the lighter path; when they want the model built AND its downstream tabs, do it all here (see § Downstream tabs).

## Research first — understand the business before modeling
**Step 0 — ASK THE USER FOR SOURCE MATERIALS BEFORE DOING ANYTHING ELSE.** On triggering this skill, the very first action is to ask the user for the company's **most recent earnings release and earnings/investor presentations** (press release, earnings presentation deck, investor supplement/financial data supplement — whatever the company publishes), unless they are already uploaded in the workspace. Do not start scoping, researching from memory, or building the sheet until these materials are in hand. They serve two mandatory purposes:
- **Learn how the company presents information and thinks about its business.** The release/deck reveals the company's own segment cut, the order and grouping of its disclosures, its preferred non-GAAP presentation, and the metrics management leads with — the model's P&L rollup, engine structure, and headline lines should mirror how MANAGEMENT frames the business, not a generic template or the GAAP cut.
- **Hunt for unique KPIs disclosed there but NOT in VAActuals/consensus.** Systematically scan the release, deck, and supplement for operating metrics the company discloses that the Visible Alpha / VAActuals tab (or Bloomberg consensus) does NOT carry — e.g. cohort/vintage stats, per-account or per-user intensity metrics, product-level volumes or attach rates, geographic or channel splits, funnel metrics, monthly operating stats. These non-consensus KPIs are prime driver candidates precisely because the Street isn't modeling them line-by-line — evaluate each as a potential engine driver and build the best ones into the driver stack (with their own input rows, %-row blocks, and sourcing). Flag in the reply which drivers came from company-only disclosure vs consensus-carried lines.

**Then research the specific company so the model reflects how THIS business actually makes money — not a generic template.** A KPI-driven model is only as good as the drivers and KPIs you choose, and those differ by company (a broker's drivers ≠ an asset manager's ≠ an exchange's). This step is mandatory and comes before scoping the sheet. Do it every time:
- **Business model & segments.** How the company earns revenue, its reportable segments, and the unit economics of each. Read the user-provided earnings release + presentations first, then the latest 10-K/10-Q/20-F business section and the most recent earnings-call transcript.
- **Key drivers & KPIs.** Identify the 5–10 operating drivers that actually move revenue and earnings, and how they combine — e.g. DARTs × commission-per-trade plus margin/cash balances × NII yield (broker); contracts/ADV × rate-per-contract (exchange); AUM roll-forward × fee rate → FRE, plus realizations → performance fees (asset manager); NII off a driven balance sheet + capital (bank). Note which the company discloses monthly vs quarterly, which management guides to, and which are company-only disclosures absent from VAActuals (per Step 0).
- **Data & consensus sources.** Confirm where actuals come from (press release, Daloopa, SEC EDGAR) and the consensus source you'll seed estimates to and that the Drivers/Qtr tabs compare against (Bloomberg BQL/BDP, or a Visible Alpha / VAActuals tab if the workbook has one). Line up the market-data tickers for any volume/price-driven lines.
- **Peers & structural quirks.** Note the closest comps and anything that will bend the build: pro-forma periods, recent M&A, segment redefinitions, share-count events, accounting changes.

Sources to use: SEC EDGAR, Daloopa, Bloomberg, company IR (releases, supplements, transcripts, investor days), and web/other research for recent developments. **Do not start the P&L skeleton until you can state, in one paragraph, this company's revenue build and its KPI set.** Carry the chosen drivers/KPIs into the P&L layout, the driver engines, and the KPI selection on the Drivers and Qtr tabs. Cite/link sources on figures per the model's sourcing rules.

## Sector variants
Default is the transactional volume × price build (brokers, exchanges, per-unit businesses). For banks & broker-dealers, asset managers, and advisory/people businesses the revenue engine is replaced (banks add a driven balance-sheet + capital layer; spread books add the vintage roll-on/roll-off engine); everything else — scaffold, monthly-in-quarter, annual SUMIFS, actual-vs-estimate duality, colors, borders, focal shapes, audit — is unchanged. Read the matching sector reference before building; translate source (press release + Bloomberg, not VA) and time grain (sequential balance rolls use prior *quarter* Ending; YoY-driven levels use the same quarter one year back).

**Retail brokers (v4)** — online brokers, neobrokers, trading apps (IBKR, HOOD, BULL, SCHW-retail, and comparable names): **read `references/retail-broker-engine.md` before building — it is mandatory for this sector.** It specifies the five-layer retail-broker cascade (user funnel & funded-account roll → customer-asset ecosystem with the NNA/market bridge → per-product transaction engines as pool × share × per-account activity × take rate → the broker NII balance/yield stack → broker expenses, other revs, and capital return), the monthly-disclosure plumbing, the canonical broker KPI set for the Drivers/Qtr tabs, and the R1–R8 acceptance additions. It layers on top of this SKILL.md's formatting/audit system, replacing the generic transactional engine with the broker-specific one.

## Core design philosophy
1. **One sheet, everything visible.** P&L at top; every driver engine BELOW it in banner-labeled sections; engine outputs feed green links up into the P&L.
2. **Actual vs. estimate duality.** ACTUAL quarters: reported totals are BLUE HARDCODES from the press release with one RED PLUG per block so components reconcile to the dollar. ESTIMATE quarters: components build bottom-up from drivers, totals become black SUMs.
3. **Monthly granularity inside quarters** — for company KPIs AND market data. Month 1/2/3 rows + quarter total/average row; actual months from a Monthly tab or staging sheet (green), latest undisclosed month blue-on-yellow.
4. **Every forecast rests on a small blue-on-yellow input** — YoY % on a driver, Δ on a ratio, beta on a rate, $-level, or capture rate. Never hardcode a black forecast number; never drive a % line with a %Y/Y growth rate.
5. **Every stock rolls forward and every seam has a check.** BoP→EoP rollforwards on balances; explicit check rows tying engines together; per-share expression on every earnings stream.

## P&L layout — business rollup with detail inside
Organize the P&L by BUSINESS, not by the GAAP line-item cut. Statement-level sections sit at column C ("Revenues", "Expenses", "Profit"); inside Revenues, the deepest detail lines sit at G (e.g. Financial Advisory, Equity UW, Debt UW / FICC, Equities / fee lines) rolling to E-level business subtotals (Investment Banking, Total Trading, Asset Mgmt, Platform), then D "Total Revenues". Expenses: F-level lines (Comp, Transaction Based, Other) → D "Operating Expenses" → D "Efficiency Ratio" (gray band) with a "# Y/Y" delta row. Profit: D PPNR → F Provision → D PT Income → E Taxes/Tax Rate (+" # Y/Y") → E Pref Dividends → C Net income → C EPS → E Diluted Shares. Key statement lines (Net income, EPS) sit OUT at C — the further left, the more headline. Every major line carries % Y/Y + the boxed Annual/% Y/Y pair in 4Q columns. Engines below still follow the same segment cut so each P&L business line has its engine.
- **Incremental Margins row** directly beneath the PT-margin block: `=Δ pretax income / Δ revenue` vs the same quarter prior year, gray computed both sides — the at-a-glance flow-through discipline check. Every model gets one; it is the fastest tell on whether the estimate columns respect the business's incremental economics.

## %-row conventions
- **% Y/Y** beneath every level line (gray computed in actuals; the blue input in estimates for YoY-driven lines).
- **% Q/Q** beneath the % Y/Y on DRIVER lines and engine levels — always gray computed, both sides; it exposes sequential seasonality when setting the %Y/Y input.
- **"# Y/Y"** (point delta, e.g. bps or pp) beneath every RATIO line (efficiency ratio, comp ratio, tax rate, NIM, margins) — computed `=(cur−prior-year)` gray in actuals; where the ratio is input-driven, the Δ IS the input. Never put a % Y/Y growth row under a percentage line.
- **"$ Y/Y" / "$ Q/Q"** dollar-delta rows beneath balance/stock lines where the DOLLAR change is the story (net new units, rate-sensitive balances, plug attribution) — gray computed, same accounting format as the level. Use alongside, not instead of, the % rows.
- **% M/M / # M/M monthly sub-blocks** on monthly-disclosed KPIs (accounts/customers, client balances, volumes): a labeled `% M/M` (and where levels matter, `# M/M`) sub-block with Month 1/2/3 rows beneath the level block, plus a `% M/M Annualized` variant where the monthly run-rate is the forecasting lens. These expose intra-quarter trajectory when setting the next quarter's inputs.

## Memo blocks
Standardized sanity-check frameworks under the expense (or any ratio-driven) section, labeled `Memo: <Ratio>` at F with a G-level stack: numerator level, denominator level, the ratio, "# Y/Y", and the boxed Annual pair. Examples: Memo: Comp Ratio (comp, revenue, comp ratio); Memo: Transaction Based Expenses. Memos are informational (gray/black), never drivers; the live input lives in the engine.
- **Every `Memo:` header label carries a lavender #CCCCFF fill** (the label cell(s) only, not the data columns) so memo blocks are visually distinct from live engine blocks at a glance. This applies to ALL memo types — ratio memos, normalized-earnings memos, read-across memos, seasoning-framework memos, presentation-bridge memos.
- **Competitor read-across memo (structured)**: when an undisclosed intra-quarter driver can be triangulated from peers that disclose earlier or at higher frequency, build a `Memo: <Driver> read-across` block: each peer's relevant metric as its own labeled row (level + % Q/Q), any proxy adjustment rows (e.g. subtract a retail-proxy peer from the blended figure), and a bolded derived-signal row the analyst actually reads. Source every peer figure per the sourcing rules. Informational only — the model's input stays a blue assumption, set WITH the read-across, never linked to it.
- **Alt-data memo**: third-party trackable data that leads a disclosed KPI (app downloads, web traffic, card data) gets its own `Memo:` block with Month 1/2/3 + Total, % Y/Y / % Q/Q, an Annual pair, and a monthly % Y/Y sub-block — staged on its own data tab if the raw history is long, per the market-data staging rules.
- **Normalized-earnings memo**: `Memo: Normalized <line>` under any line distorted by rate/mark noise — restate it at a normalized-yield or normalized-ratio input (blue-on-yellow, e.g. "alts yield normalized at 11%"), show the normalized level, the normalized margin, and the headline-vs-normalized gap. The normalization input is an assumption cell; the memo never feeds the P&L.

## Scenario memos
Stress cases live as labeled MEMO rows directly under the line they stress — never as separate tabs and never as drivers. Label `Memo: <line> — Downside ('08-style)` / `Base` / `Bear` at F, with the stressed level and the delta vs base beneath. Typical uses: credit losses at prior-crisis severity, flow downside cases, buyback base/bear paths. Scenario rows are informational (gray/black with blue-on-yellow scenario inputs); the live P&L runs off the base-case drivers only. Flag in the reply which lines carry scenario memos.

## Guidance overlay (optional — only if a "Guidance" tab exists)
If the workbook has a tab named "Guidance," annotate the Model with what management has told the Street, so "what they said" sits directly beneath "what they delivered" (actual columns) and "what they're modeled to deliver" (estimate columns) — said-vs-delivered at a glance, backward and forward. **If there is NO "Guidance" tab, skip this entirely** and build the Model normally.
- **Read the tab adaptively** — it has no fixed layout. Understand its structure (flat list, or metric × period matrix) and extract, per item, the (line/metric, period, guidance-as-stated) triple plus any source/date. Preserve guidance verbatim — keep ranges, inequalities, units, qualifiers; never round a range to a point or restate qualitative guidance.
- **Match each item to a P&L line by label** (semantic, not exact-string). Attach segment guidance to the segment line, not the total. Note basis mismatches (adjusted guide vs GAAP line). List anything unmatched or ambiguous in the reply — never silently drop it.
- **Building fresh — plan the guidance rows into the P&L skeleton BEFORE the map is frozen** (build order step 4): one annotation row directly beneath each guided line's complete block, so all row numbers are final from the start and nothing ever has to be inserted. Don't hand-insert rows into a finished sheet — openpyxl won't shift formulas, so late insertion silently breaks engine links, the annual SUMIFS, the EPS strip, and every downstream-tab reference.
- **Place the guidance period-aligned** — each figure in the COLUMN of the period it pertains to (FY guide → that annual column; quarterly guide → that quarter column), sitting under the model's own number for that period. Store as a string so ranges/qualifiers render faithfully (text is ignored by the row's SUMIFS); period-agnostic guidance goes left-aligned in the label area. Guidance is a display annotation — it never feeds a formula.
- **Format**: violet #7030A0 italic, Calibri 9pt; row label "Mgmt Guidance" at the line's indent (date-stamp it — one row per vintage — if the tab tracks guidance revisions). **NO embedded cell notes/comments on guidance cells** — keep guidance cells clean; put the source (call/release + date) visibly in the row label or a small gray source cell in the label area, and list sources in the reply. EXCLUDE guidance rows from the subtotal/driver border sweep and the Annual box draw (same exclusion as Annual-pair rows) so no rules cut through them.
- **Overlaying onto a model that already exists** (or re-running guidance after the Model's map is frozen) — you can no longer reserve rows, so use the bundled **`scripts/insert_guidance_rows.py`**: it inserts the guidance rows AND repairs every A1 reference across the whole workbook (Model + downstream tabs — sheet-qualified refs, `$` anchors, and range endpoints whose tail inherits the head's sheet are all handled), so nothing breaks. Then recalc and confirm every anchor (subtotals, EPS, downstream links) is unchanged before delivering. This is the ONLY safe way to insert into a finished model — never hand-insert.
Full mechanics for BOTH paths (fresh-build reservation and the bundled helper), matching rules, the helper's JSON spec, edge cases, and a worked example: read `references/guidance-overlay.md`.

## Market-data tracking blocks & TAM → share ladders
When a revenue engine is driven by MARKET data (industry volumes, value traded, rates), build a tracking block with full provenance, directly above the line it drives:
- **Region/market sub-header** at E (e.g. "US"), one block per region if multi-region.
- **Month 1 / Month 2 / Month 3 rows + a quarterly Average row** (monthly-in-quarter applies to market data, not just company KPIs). Quarterly-only history may populate just the Average row.
- **Source-URL row** directly under the block header (the stats page the data comes from) — italic gray.
- **Column K tags**: a short source tag ("SIFMA") in column K beside every hand-tracked data row; the Bloomberg ticker string ("MVOLUSE Index") in column K beside every live-pull row so the ticker is auditable next to the data.
- **Every web-sourced hardcode carries a cell note** with source name + URL (and the derivation if the figure was derived from stated Q/Q / Y/Y / YTD figures). Unsourced gaps stay BLANK blue inputs — never interpolate silently; flag them in the reply.
- **Bloomberg staging sheet**: long BDH histories live on a dedicated staging sheet, one ticker per column pair (date, value) with the ticker in the header row; the Model block references/aggregates it. Keep raw pull history off the Model tab. Where Bloomberg is unavailable, leave live rows erroring or blank with a note — never paste static numbers pretending to be live.
- Dash-tolerant guards (`IFERROR(...,"-")`, `IF(x="","-",…)`) on every computed row over gappy market history so gaps never propagate #DIV/0!.
- **TAM → share ladder**: when the company's line is a share of an industry pool, build the full ladder above it — (1) industry TAM / pool level (sourced, with tracking block); (2) company share row — gray computed `=company/industry` in history, the blue LEVEL input in estimates; (3) company line `=share × pool`. Optionally add: a **competitor share table** (top-N players' volumes/share, sourced, with K-tags) so the share input is set against named competitors; **readacross memos** from other tickers that disclose earlier (labeled `Memo: <ticker> readacross`, red italic consensus/benchmark convention); and for international TAMs an **FX row** (local-currency TAM × sourced FX rate) so the translation is visible. Ladder memos are informational; only the share input drives.

## Per-unit intensity blocks
For any business with a countable customer/unit base (accounts, clients, members, subscribers, seats, locations), express every major balance and activity line as a per-unit intensity and FORECAST the intensity, not the raw level:
- Beneath (or beside) each major balance/activity block, a `<Line> per <unit>` block: Month 1/2/3 where monthly-disclosed, EOP (or annualized total for flows), % Y/Y, % Q/Q, and a Seasonals row where the intensity is seasonal (e.g. activity per unit annualized). Deep-detail intensity blocks may indent one level further (H–J labels) than their parent line.
- In estimates, the intensity carries the blue input (level or Δ) and the line rebuilds as `intensity × units` — separating unit growth from unit economics so each is set and wiggle-tested independently. Typical set: balance per unit (equity/AUM/deposits per account), activity per unit (trades/volume per account, annualized), monetization per unit (revenue or fee $ per account).
- Where an intensity is derived rather than driving, keep it as a gray computed block with a small `EOP <Units>` / `EOP <Balance>` scaffold above it so the division is auditable.

## Market-vs-organic bridge & balance seasoning framework
Any client balance that moves with markets (client equity, AUM, custody/brokerage assets) gets its monthly/quarterly change DECOMPOSED into market impact vs organic flows — never forecast such a balance with a single %Y/Y:
- **Market Assumptions block**: the relevant index (ticker as the block's sourced row label) with Month 1/2/3 levels, a quarter row, % Y/Y / % Q/Q, and a `% M/M` sub-block. Actual months hardcode blue from published levels; estimate months run off blue % M/M (or flat-level) inputs.
- **Decomposition sub-blocks** beneath the balance: `# M/M from market returns` (= BoP balance × levered index return), `# M/M total change`, and `# M/M Change ex Markets` (organic) with a quarterly total, % Q/Q, and a Seasonality row — organic flows are what the analyst actually forecasts; market impact rides the index block.
- **`Memo: <Balance> Seasoning Framework`** (CCCCFF memo header): an implied-EOP bridge that rebuilds the balance bottom-up as a sanity check — BoP balance, less non-market components (cash/credit balances), = BoP invested balance; × levered market return (leverage ratio × index % Q/Q + an alpha/outperformance input); plus new-unit funding (new units × initial funding per unit, blue inputs); plus backbook adds ($ adder per existing unit × backbook units); plus the non-market components back — → **Implied EOP balance vs Model EOP balance with an `Implied vs Modeled` check row** (red italic; small persistent gaps are the seasoning signal, large gaps are a build error). All memo inputs blue-on-yellow; the memo never feeds the live balance.

## Sheet grid & header scaffold
- Columns A–G indent; H–I label helpers; J–K main label columns (K doubles as the source/ticker tag column in data blocks); L… one column per quarter; after a spacer, ANNUAL columns.
- Freeze panes below the period banner row / after col K (see § Period banner row). Gridlines OFF.
- Scaffold rows: month-end EOMONTH chains; quarter-end date; days in quarter; annualization factor; Bloomberg period strings; fiscal-year integer (SUMIFS key); quarter label ("1Q"/"2Q"/…); EPS summary strip (§ EPS strip); the period banner (§ Period banner row). **Banner titles are terse** ("Trading", not "TRADING — GLOBAL MARKETS").
- **Bloomberg period strings are DERIVED from the banner label, never typed**: two scaffold rows per estimate column parse the quarter label into the BQL/BDP period arguments (e.g. from "3Q26" → a `FPR`-style string `=+"20"&MID(<label>,3,2)&…` and a `"3QFY-2026"`-style string `=+LEFT(<label>,2)&"FY-20"&MID(<label>,3,2)`), so every consensus pull re-periods automatically when columns roll.
- **Bloomberg canary cell**: one small red live cell in the scaffold label area (e.g. `=BDP("IBM US Equity","PX_LAST")`) whose only job is to show at a glance whether the Bloomberg link is alive — a stale/erroring canary explains erroring consensus rows immediately.
- Mechanical scaffold memo rows (day count, annualization factor, avg-price memos) are italic #333F4F, CENTERED in the data columns, accounting format.
- Where trading days matter, compute them with NETWORKDAYS off the quarter-end row rather than calendar days.

## Period banner row (v6 — exact spec)
The single dark-green period-header row (row 21 in the IBKR reference) is the visual spine of the model. Get it exactly right:
- **Placement & freeze**: it is the LAST row of the frozen header block — freeze panes sit immediately below it and after column K, so the banner and its period labels stay in view at any scroll position. Compact row height (~12pt).
- **Fill & font**: #375623 fill, white bold, base font (Calibri 9). The fill runs CONTINUOUSLY from column B through the last quarter column, BREAKS at the spacer column(s), then resumes over the annual columns. Column A stays unfilled.
- **Alignment**: left-aligned over the label area (B:J); right-aligned in K and in every quarter and annual column.
- **Labels are formulas, never typed**: each quarter banner cell concatenates the scaffold rows — `=<quarter-label cell>&<fiscal-year cell>` → "1Q16", "4Q25" — so relabeling a year propagates automatically. Annual banner cells show the fiscal-year INTEGER (2022, 2023, …) linked/incremented from the scaffold, not text.
- **White fiscal-year separator rules**: MEDIUM WHITE (#FFFFFF) vertical borders inside the banner group each fiscal year into a 4-quarter block — (a) right edge of column K (before the first quarter); (b) left edge of every 1Q column AND right edge of every 4Q column (same gridline — set both edges so the rule survives either neighbor being reformatted); (c) left edge of the first column after the last quarter (the spacer seam). Draw them on the banner row only.
- **Maintenance**: when quarter columns are appended, extend the fill and label formulas and RE-DRAW the year separators; verify by image — separator and fill correctness cannot be confirmed from value reads.
- **Section banners** deeper in the sheet are two-tier (§ Borders & structure): major engine sections reuse this dark-green fill/white bold font at B; sub-sections use light-gold #FFF2CC / black bold at C. Neither carries year separators — those belong to the period banner only.

## EPS strip (v6 — exact spec)

## Rate-path scaffold
When floating-rate exposure or a cuts/hikes narrative matters (banks, insurers, brokers with cash sweep), build a policy-rate path block in the rates engine:
- **Per-month Δbps inputs** (blue-on-yellow, e.g. −0.25% on cut months, 0 otherwise) under a Month 1/2/3 structure.
- **Cumulative-change counter** row summing Δs from the path start.
- **Monthly rate chain** `=prior month + Δ`; **quarterly average** row off the three months — the average, not the EOP, drives yield lines.
- Actual months hardcode blue from the published fixings; estimate months run off the Δ inputs.
- Floating-rate revenue/cost lines = floating balance × (rate average + spread); pair with a fixed/floating **exposure split row** so the mix is explicit. Betas remain valid for administered-rate products; the rate path replaces betas where repricing is mechanical.
- **Live-rates block layout**: a "Rates — BB Live Formulas" sub-section heads the NII engine, one row per benchmark (policy rate, foreign rates) with Month 1/2/3 + a quarterly **Average** row; the Bloomberg TICKER STRING sits as a row label in the left label area directly beside each live row (in addition to the K-tag convention) so every live series is auditable in place.
- **Blended multi-currency rate mix** (global balance books): per-currency rate rows (each riding its own forward curve / rate path), blue **mix-weight inputs** per currency, and a computed **blended rate** row = Σ(mix × rate) that drives the yield lines; beta blocks then express each product's rate as **cum-beta / as-a-%-of-the-blended (or policy) rate** rows so repricing assumptions are explicit.
- **Rate-paying vs non-rate-paying balance split**: where the company only pays (or earns full) yield above a threshold or on a subset of balances, split the balance into the rate-sensitive and non-rate-sensitive components (each with its own per-unit intensity, %/$ delta rows and seasonals) and drive interest off the rate-sensitive piece only, with a `Memo: % of balances earning full yield` (CCCCFF header) tracking the mix.
- **NII presentation bridge**: when the engine's economic NII differs from the company's reported NII line (fee reclasses, one-timers), reconcile explicitly beneath the engine — engine NII, `Less:`/`Plus:` each presentation item, → **Reported NII** (the line the P&L and consensus tie to), with %Y/Y / %Q/Q and the Annual pair on the reported row. Never let the P&L link an unreconciled engine total.

- Blue on #FFFFCC = forward ASSUMPTION inputs in estimate columns only; never on a formula cell.
- Green #008000 = links (engine output → P&L, cross-tab pulls). Red #FF0000 italic = plugs, Bloomberg live pulls, consensus/benchmark memos, check rows (red OVERRIDES green). Red on #FFCCCC = BS cash plug.
- Black = calcs. Gray #F2F2F2 fill = headline ratio bands + the EPS strip band. Gray #7F7F7F italic = computed %Y/Y-, %Q/Q-, #Y/Y- and ratio memos. Italic #333F4F centered = mechanical memos (day counts, annualization). Brown #833C0C = seasonality memos. #D9D9D9 (or #B2B2B2) font = parked/de-emphasized helper and commentary labels (visible but receding).

- **Block headers are bold + single-underline**: the header label of every statement section and driven block ("Revenues" at C, "Commissions" at D, engine block titles) carries bold AND a single font underline — the underline marks "a block builds to this line".
- Number formats: $mm accounting `_(* #,##0_);_(* (#,##0);_(* "-"?_);_(@_)` (single `?` pad on whole-number rows — use this exact string); 2-dp accounting `_(* #,##0.00_);_(* (#,##0.00);_(* "-"??_);_(@_)` (`??` pads dashes under decimals); growth `0.0%;(0.0%);-`; rates `0.00%`; EPS `$#,##0.00`; shares `#,##0.0`; per-unit capture `$#,##0.00` or `$#,##0.000`; dates `m/d/yyyy`; VA/Daloopa whole-percent ratios `0.000` (divide by 100 against a base).

## Label indentation
The further left, the more headline: C statement lines (Revenues, Expenses, Profit, Net income, EPS) → D key totals / engine outputs feeding the P&L → E business subtotals / primary lines → F sub-components → G deepest detail and operating-driver rows. % rows sit in the same column as (or one right of) their line, with leading spaces. Engine labels mirror the P&L hierarchy.

## Driver placement
- Calculating/summing drivers go ABOVE the line they produce; the % Y/Y input goes BELOW the level it drives; % Q/Q below that.
- Drivers are inline directly above the line they build — never parked in a top KPI block. Shared drivers repeat with link-back to the first instance; calendar metrics live once in the scaffold.
- Driver-block top-rule: thin black top border on every driver-fed line.

## Rollforwards & checks
- **Every stock rolls forward**: AUM, invested assets, deposits, book equity, share count — `BoP (=prior EoP) + inflows/additions − outflows/reductions ± market/other = EoP`, with each flow line driven by its own input (% of BoP rates, engine links, or $-levels). Never let a balance jump between quarters without a rollforward.
- **Inflow sources split**: separate captive/affiliate inflows, third-party inflows, and new-initiative ramps as distinct rows so each carries its own driver.
- **Check rows at every seam**: wherever two engines must agree (rollforward total vs flows block, sum of segments vs consolidated, equity build vs balance sheet, capital generated vs capital deployed+returned), add an explicit check row `=side A − side B` that must show 0/"-". Format check rows red italic like tie memos; label them `Check: <what ties>`. A feasibility check on capital (does the forecast generate enough capital to fund the assumed growth + payout?) is mandatory for balance-sheet businesses.
- Zero-valued check rows are part of the final audit: any nonzero check fails the build.

## Up-C / two-tier tax & NCI variant (only when the structure applies)
When the public company owns a minority-to-majority slice of an operating partnership/sub (Up-C, HoldCo/OpCo, listed-sub structures), never model taxes and NCI as single blended rates — build the two-tier waterfall in the Tax & NCI engine:
- **Tier 1 (operating sub)**: PT income → op-sub tax (own rate row + "# Y/Y") → **Net income to members**.
- **Tier 2 (public HoldCo)**: net income to members × **ownership %** (an average-ownership row for the P&L plus an annual-ownership row; ownership rolls with buybacks/exchanges) → income attributable to the public co, less HoldCo-level tax (own rate row) → **Net income to common** — the EPS numerator.
- **NCI cross-check block**: rebuild NCI independently (total NI ± HoldCo-only items, × the outside-ownership share) → `Implied NCI` bold, with a red **Plug** row `=NI − NI to common − implied NCI` that must sit at ~0 — the two-tier build and the reported NCI must agree.
- **Public-ownership share block** in the share section: public/HoldCo diluted shares vs total (all-classes/units) diluted shares, public ownership % with an `Annual # Q/Q` row — the ownership input the waterfall consumes.
- **Dividend block**: `Div per share (blue input) × shares out = Total divs paid`, feeding the equity-roll `Less: Divs` line so payout is driven, not hardcoded, in the balance sheet.

## Per-share discipline
- **Every segment earnings stream gets a per-share row**: segment income/share, pre-tax income/share, FCF/share, etc. — `=stream / diluted shares`, gray computed, beneath the stream's Annual pair. EPS remains the headline C-level line.
- **Share count**: seed diluted shares from consensus (`cons NI / cons EPS`). Optionally add a **share-count roll engine** in the capital-return section: `BoP shares − repurchased (=buyback $ / avg price, with avg price a blue input or live pull) + issued (SBC input) = EoP shares`, weighted-average line feeding EPS. If the roll is built, the consensus-seeded share count becomes the tie/seed target — back-solve the buyback $ so shares tie consensus at seed; a check row ties the roll to the consensus-implied count. Never approximate diluted shares as an ad-hoc average of unrelated share rows.

## Borders & structure
- Subtotal top/bottom rules span **from the row's label column through the last data column** — covering the label text and indent cells, continuous with no gaps. In monthly blocks this means a thin bottom rule under Month 3 and a thin top rule on the Total row, both spanning label→numbers. Sweep mechanics: for each non-pair row read EdgeTop/EdgeBottom over the data range; `Continuous` or `null` (mixed) → re-apply uniformly across `{labelCol}:{lastDataCol}`. Skip banner rows; EXCLUDE Annual pair rows (find by scanning labels for "Annual"; pair = that row + the next) — a full-width sweep draws lines through their boxes.
- **Annual + % Y/Y boxed pairs on the P&L AND every engine subtotal/output line**, in 4Q columns only: Annual `=SUM(4 trailing qtrs)` bold (EOP stocks link the 4Q value), % Y/Y vs the prior year's annual below, labels in the parent's column. **Draw each box as ONE 2-cell range setting all four edges at once, with a two-phase clear-then-box write** (clear all pair-row borders full-width, sync, then draw boxes, sync) — a cell's right edge is its neighbor's left edge, and single-pass clearing wipes box right borders.
- The period banner's white year-separator rules (§ Period banner row) are the only MEDIUM-weight, only WHITE borders on the sheet; everything else is thin black.
- Focal columns are SHAPES (weight 1, y=0, full model height) — one on the focal quarter, one on the focal annual column; resize after row inserts.
- **Section banners are TWO-TIER**: (1) MAJOR engine sections (the B-level cuts: e.g. the transactional engine, NII, Tax & NCI, Cash Flows, Balance Sheet) get the dark-green #375623 row, white bold, terse title at B; (2) SUB-SECTIONS inside an engine (the C-level cuts: e.g. Accounts, each product engine, Rates, IEA, Liabilities, Yields, Betas, FCF calc) get a **light-gold #FFF2CC row, black bold, title at C**. Neither tier carries year separators. A model whose engines all sit under one flat banner tier is a formatting error.

## Actuals must tie to the dollar
Reported totals blue-hardcoded in actual columns, one red plug per block; NI ties to reported NI-applicable-to-common with a red $0 tie-memo row.

## Estimates — seed to consensus, then sensitize
Every estimate level is a formula off a flexible input; every seed back-solved from the matching consensus figure (`%YY_seed = consensus / SameQtrPriorYr − 1`; ETR back-solved from consensus NI; diluted shares = cons NI / cons EPS). Plugs & reconciliation lines are hardcoded blue-on-yellow inputs in estimate columns (a live-link plug neutralizes drivers). **Market data has no consensus** — seed market-volume %Y/Y to a transparent stated assumption (e.g. hold the latest run-rate, flat thereafter) and FLAG it; back-solve the capture/share input so the revenue line still ties consensus at seed. Re-run the wiggle test after converting any plug.

## Forecast mechanisms — reference
- **Operating-driver lines**: `volume × price × days / scale`; driver levels `=SameQtrPriorYr*(1+%YY)` with blue %YY below.
- **Market-pool × capture lines**: revenue = total market volume (from the tracking block: ADV × trading days, or value traded) × a $-capture rate. Capture is computed gray in history (`=revenue/volume`) and a blue LEVEL input in estimates, back-solved to consensus at seed. Use when the company's line rides an industry pool it doesn't disclose drivers for (market-making, brokerage, clearing). With a TAM → share ladder, the share row plays the capture role. The detail split lines beneath become informational memos once the total is pool-driven — note it.
- **Vintage roll-on/roll-off yield engine** — for any spread book with repricing lag (fixed-income portfolios, annuity/deposit books, loan books): split the balance into floating (rides the rate-path scaffold) and fixed. For the fixed book each quarter: **roll-off yield** = trailing average of the NEW-MONEY yield from N years back (N = the book's average duration — the maturing vintage's yield, `=AVERAGE` over the 4 quarters ending N years prior); **roll-on yield** = current new-money yield (tracked/sourced line with its own driver); portfolio yield $ = `prior + roll-on × additions − roll-off × maturities`, /4 for the quarterly P&L. Apply SYMMETRICALLY to asset yields and cost of funds (funding roll uses product-level rates, e.g. tracked annuity/deposit rates with Month 1/2/3 blocks). New-money yield history must be long enough to reach back N years — extend it or flag the gap.
- **Headcount × comp-per-employee engine** — default comp build for people businesses, optional elsewhere: employees roll `=prior + net new hires` (blue net-adds input); comp-per-employee level `=prior × (1 + %Q/Q input)` with a Seasonal benchmark row beneath (comp resets seasonally — Q1 resets, Q4 true-ups); comp = `employees × comp/employee / scale`. Productivity memos beneath: revenue (or capital deployed) per employee, LTM, comp ratio TTM. Seed the %Q/Q input so LTM comp ratio ties consensus/guided ratio.
- **YoY%-driven lines**: level `=SameQtrPriorYr*(1+%YY)`, blue %YY input.
- **Ratios**: Δ/level inputs only. Subtotals: explicit cell sums, never `=SUM(rangeWithGrowthRows)`; use `SUM(cell,cell,…)` over text dashes.
- **EPS basis**: match the consensus basis before building any GAAP-vs-operating bridge; never divide model EPS by a gap row.

## Annual columns
Flows `=SUMIFS($L<row>:$<last><row>, $L$14:$<last>$14, <yearCell>)`; EOP stocks link the 4Q value; ratios recompute from annual numerator/denominator; averages via AVERAGEIFS; ADV-type metrics = annual volume / annual days. %Y/Y columns off the prior annual column (skip the first). **The SUMIFS range is ONE fixed range, identical in every annual column and every row — never a per-column patched range.** It must cover every quarter column of every fiscal year shown in the annual block; it may START at the first quarter of the EARLIEST displayed annual year (rather than the model's first quarter) and may run through the spacer column harmlessly (reference pattern: `=SUMIFS($X<row>:$BL<row>,$X$14:$BL$14,BN$14)` where quarters run L:BK and annuals start at 2022=X). A range that stops short of the last quarter column silently drops quarters from the annuals. When quarter columns are added, extend the range everywhere in one pass and re-verify annuals.
